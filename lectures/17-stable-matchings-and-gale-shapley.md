## Goals
- Run Gale-Shapley and prove it terminates with a stable matching.

## Key concepts
- Two sides with preference lists; a matching is **stable** if no pair prefers each other to their partners.
- **Gale-Shapley**: free proposers propose in preference order; receivers keep the best offer so far.
- Terminates in at most `n²` proposals; the result is stable and proposer-optimal.
